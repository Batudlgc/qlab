maker    every_10   every_50  every_step

0.000        3169       1070        3876
0.005        3169       1070        3876
0.010        3169       1070        3876
0.020        3169       1070        3876

The rebate is paid per unit resting-side filled, so it scales with volume,
not with edge. That is precisely why it changes who is profitable: the
high-volume maker collects the most rebate regardless of whether its
fills were any good. A strategy that is only viable at a given rebate
tier is a strategy whose edge is the rebate.
