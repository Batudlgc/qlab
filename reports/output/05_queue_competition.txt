Cancelling to refresh forfeits FIFO priority; holding a quote keeps the
priority but leaves a stale price exposed. Compare markout_vs_informed
across the three: that column is the price of staleness.

EXPERIMENT D - inventory skew, studied deliberately (cap = 50)
========================================================================================================================
            flow_share  total_pnl  spread_capture  inventory_pnl      units  markout_per_unit  markout_vs_informed  avg_queue_ahead
maker

skew_0.01        0.404     71.316         263.259       -191.943   3173.200             0.023               -0.196            1.156

Skew x max_inventory must stay small relative to the spread you quote,
or inventory management silently becomes your pricing policy.
Here 0.01 x 50 = 0.50 against a half-spread of 0.10 - the tail wagging the dog.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Summary of what is and is not established:
  established : price priority dominates queue priority; staleness is paid
                for in adverse selection; skew can swamp quoted spread.
  NOT established : anything about latency, fees, or maker rebates -
                none of which this simulation models.
